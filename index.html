Praveer Singh, Ph.D.
Assistant Professor, Department of Ophthalmology

I am an Assistant Professor at the University of Colorado School of Medicine in the Department of Ophthalmology, and an Honorary Researcher at Moorfields Eye Hospital, UK.

My research focuses on Artificial Intelligence in Ophthalmology, Neonatology, and Radiology, with particular emphasis on Federated/Collaborative Learning, Bias/Fairness in AI, Trustworthiness of AI models, and Natural Language Processing. I work on developing AI systems for medical imaging that are both accurate and ethically sound, with applications in retinal disease detection and systemic disease prediction.

I am interested in collaborating with researchers and clinicians working on AI applications in medical imaging. If you are interested in potential collaborations or have questions about my research, please feel free to reach out via email.

Research & Mentorship

Current Students & Mentees:

  • Shivang Mistry, BASc, University of Toronto, Toronto, CA - "Age as a confounding factor in training Deep Learning models for neurodegenerative diseases from retinal images" (Work accepted at ARVO 2025)
  • Multiple undergraduate and graduate students working on AI applications in ophthalmology and medical imaging

Research Areas:

  • AI in Ophthalmology/Neonatology: Developed visualization techniques for Deep Learning model validation on external datasets, AI-based synthetic medical dataset generation for privacy preservation, ROP stage prediction, severe ROP risk prediction, and systemic disease prediction from retinal fundus photographs.
  • Multi-Institutional Collaborative Learning: Built real-time collaborative learning techniques without sharing patient data and proposed methods to avoid catastrophic forgetting when fine-tuning on new medical datasets.
  • Tracking Disease Severity: AI algorithms for continuous disease severity evaluation including assessment of progression in various retinal conditions.
  • Bias and Fairness in Medical AI: Research on ensuring equitable AI systems across diverse patient populations.
